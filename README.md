**Project Description – BioPipe, Bioplastic and Biochar**

Our project focuses on converting ornamental plant waste, agricultural residues and discarded flowers into valuable, eco-friendly products such as biodegradable BioPipes, bioplastics and biochar. Natural fibres and plant-based materials are used to develop sustainable alternatives to conventional plastics, while organic waste can be converted into biochar through controlled heating in limited oxygen. Our aim is to reduce waste, minimize plastic pollution, promote recycling and support a greener, sustainable future.
